Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question that is not here? Ask it on your review. That is what it is for.

What does a Medicare Supplement plan do?

It helps pay costs that Original Medicare leaves to you, like the 20% coinsurance for Part B care and, depending on the plan, your deductibles. It works alongside Original Medicare. Some people call it Medigap.

Is it the same as Medicare Advantage?

No. Medicare Advantage is a different way to get your Medicare, usually with a network of doctors. A Supplement works with Original Medicare. You cannot use both at the same time.

When is the best time to get one?

During your Medigap Open Enrollment Period: the 6 months that start the month you are 65 or older and enrolled in Part B. In that window, companies cannot turn you down or charge you more because of your health.

Do Supplement plans cover prescriptions?

No. Plans sold today do not include drug coverage, so most people pair a Supplement with a separate Part D plan.

Does the review cost anything?

No. The plan review is free, and there is no obligation to buy anything.
